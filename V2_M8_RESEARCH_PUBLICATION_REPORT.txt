1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-------------------
Milestone M8 establishes an automated, living research publishing pipeline for
Social Gravity. In computational social science and network physics, reproducibility
crises often stem from unpinned PRNG seeds, opaque hyperparameters, and manual data
wrangling. 

The `PublicationPipeline` programmatically generates:
1. Automated Reproducibility Manifests: Tracks Node environment, V8 runtime,
   SplitMix32 PRNG seed states, dataset SHA-256 checksums, and model quantization.
2. Publication-Ready LaTeX Tables: Formatted for direct copy-paste into ACM / IEEE /
   AAAI / NeurIPS conference templates:
   - Prediction Accuracy Table (MAE, RMSE, Brier score, Recall)
   - Scaled Performance Table (Nodes, Edges, Gen ms, Sim ms, Throughput, Heap)
3. Academic Citation Blocks: Complete BibTeX citations targeted at top-tier venues
   (ICWSM, The Web Conference, AAAI).
