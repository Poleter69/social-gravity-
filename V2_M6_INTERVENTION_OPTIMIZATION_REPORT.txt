1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-------------------
Milestone M6 introduces algorithmic intervention optimization to replace manual,
predefined countermeasures with an automated Pareto frontier search. In active
crises, security and risk operators face hard resource limits: communicating with
influencers or briefing executive spokespeople carries operational costs. 

The `InterventionOptimizer` evaluates the tradeoff between intervention budget
(number and tier of nodes engaged) and epidemic containment efficiency, discovering
the set of non-dominated Pareto-optimal strategies.

Key Achievements:
- Automated Strategy Space Search: Evaluates minimal bridge pinpointing,
  multi-bridge perimeter inoculation, single-hub counter-voices, full influencer
  broadcasts, and hybrid coalition strategies.
- Pareto Frontier Filter: Discards dominated options (strategies that cost more
  yet achieve lower or equal containment).
- Cost-Benefit Metric: Quantifies operational cost per percentage point of
  containment achieved.
- Automated Policy Recommendation: Selects the highest-efficiency plan on the
  frontier for immediate operational execution.
