1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-------------------
Milestone M1 transforms Social Gravity from a retrospective simulation runner into
an operational forward forecasting engine. Rather than simply observing how a
rumor has diffused up to round T, the Forecast Engine now projects future diffusion
trajectories across a rolling forecast horizon (e.g. +6 to +15 ticks).

Key Capabilities Introduced:
- Rolling Forecast Horizon: Multi-step parallel stochastic ensemble projection.
- Probabilistic Confidence Intervals: 5th, 50th (median), and 95th percentiles
  for believer count, effective reproduction number R0, and population adoption rate.
- Cross-Community Contagion Early Warning: Automated early warning statement
  quantifying probability of boundary-crossing contagion (e.g., "78% probability
  of cross-community spread within 6 ticks (Confidence: 86.4%)").
- Trend Extrapolation: Computes cascade momentum (first derivative) and
  acceleration (second derivative) with peak timing projections.
- Uncertainty Decomposition: Separates aleatoric uncertainty (stochastic PRNG
  seed variance) from epistemic uncertainty (psychological trait heterogeneity).
- Deterministic Replay: 100% bit-for-bit equivalence under identical branched PRNG seeds.


2. ARCHITECTURE & IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------
- Primary Types: `src/forecasting/types.ts`
  * `ConfidenceInterval`: `{ lower: number; median: number; upper: number; confidenceLevel: number }`
  * `TickForecast`: Per-tick projected metrics, adoption bounds, and risk classification.
  * `EarlyWarningStatement`: Cross-community spread probability, target tick, confidence score.
  * `TrendExtrapolation`: Momentum, acceleration, peak round, and percolation flags.
  * `UncertaintyDecomposition`: Aleatoric, epistemic, and total uncertainty quantification.
  * `ForecastReport`: Unified export container.

- Engine Core: `src/forecasting/forecastEngine.ts`
  * `ForecastEngine.generateForecast(baseEngine, horizonRounds, ensembleSize, seedBase)`
  * Spawns an ensemble of cloned simulation states with deterministically derived seeds.
  * Measures cross-community edge crossings at each discrete step.
  * Sorts values across ensemble runs to calculate robust non-parametric percentiles.
