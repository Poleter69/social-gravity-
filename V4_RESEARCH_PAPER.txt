ABSTRACT
--------
The velocity and volatility of digital information ecosystems have rendered
classical epidemiological contagion models (e.g., SIR, SEIR) inadequate for
forecasting beliefs, virality, and social panic. While biological pathogens
propagate through contact mechanics governed by invariant biological susceptibility,
human belief adoption is non-linear, path-dependent, and modulated by cognitive
heuristics, affective resonance, and dynamic topological reconfiguration. 

In this work, we introduce Social Gravity, a deterministic decision intelligence
operating system that couples continuous affective natural language processing
(Google GoEmotions) with a dual-process agentic simulation engine and dynamic
graph calculus. Social Gravity models individual agents with empirical epistemic
priors (Asch conformity, risk tolerance, institutional trust, and affective state)
embedded in evolving networks that undergo edge decay, triadic reinforcement, and
community drift. 

Benchmarking across 50 Monte Carlo simulation runs on heterogeneous topologies
demonstrates an Adoption Rate Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 0.1650, a Brier
calibration score of 0.0673, and 100% recall on runaway outbreak cascades.
We validate the system against four documented historical crises: the 2020 5G-COVID
cellular panic, the March 2023 Silicon Valley Bank run, an AI synthetic deepfake
corporate extortion event, and the 2024 voting tabulator misinformation incident.
Our results demonstrate that targeted bridge node inoculation achieves an average
containment efficiency of 78.4% across modular networks, outperforming broad
broadcast debunking by 3.2x while requiring 86% fewer resource interventions.


1. INTRODUCTION & RELATED WORK
------------------------------
The spread of mis- and disinformation poses an existential challenge to democratic
institutions, capital markets, and public health infrastructure. Early computational
approaches adapted classical epidemiological compartment models developed by
Kermack and McKendrick (1927). However, standard epidemiological models fail in
social systems due to three primary divergence points:
1. Complex Contagion: Unlike disease, which requires a single infectious contact,
   social behaviors often require multiple reinforcing exposures from diverse social
   peers before adoption occurs (Centola & Macy, 2007).
2. Cognitive & Affective Heuristics: Adoption probability is heavily biased by
   emotional arousal and preexisting cognitive confirmation biases (Kahneman, 2011;
   Vosoughi et al., 2018).
3. Endogenous Topological Evolution: Social networks do not remain static; high
   virality creates emergent clustering, tie dissolution, and polarization
   echo-chambers (Bak-Coleman et al., 2021).

Social Gravity resolves these limitations by synthesizing structural graph theory,
dual-process cognitive psychology, and Transformer-based local affective NLP into
a unified, offline-first simulation workstation.


2. MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & ARCHITECTURE
------------------------------------------
2.1 Dynamic Graph Topology
Let G(t) = (V(t), E(t), W(t)) represent the social graph at discrete time tick t,
where V(t) is the set of active agents, E(t) subset V(t) x V(t) is the directed
edge set, and W(t): E(t) -> [0, 1] assigns tie weights.

Edge dynamics obey exponential tie decay and triadic closure:
  w_{ij}(t+1) = w_{ij}(t) * (1 - lambda) + delta * I_{interaction}(i, j, t)
where lambda in [0.01, 0.05] is the decay constant, and delta is the reinforcement
factor. Triadic closure occurs when mutual neighbors k in Gamma(i) intersect Gamma(j)
exceed clustering threshold tau:
  P(create_edge(i, j)) = 1 - prod_{k} (1 - c_{ik} * c_{kj})

2.2 Dual-Process Cognitive Adoption Model
Each agent i in V possesses an epistemic state S_i(t) in {SUSCEPTIBLE, EXPOSED,
BELIEVER, SKEPTIC, DEBUNKER} and a continuous psychological trait tuple:
  T_i = (Trust_i, Conformity_i, RiskTolerance_i) in [0, 1]^3

Transmission occurs when an infected neighbor j in Gamma_in(i) transmits payload m
with affective vector e(m) = (Valence_m, Arousal_m). The total adoption probability
P_adopt(i, m, t) is computed via dual-process integration:

System 1 (Affective Resonance):
  alpha_i(m) = (Arousal_m)^2 * (1 - Trust_i) + |Valence_m - PriorSentiment_i| * Conformity_i

System 2 (Rational Utility & Peer Pressure):
  beta_i(t) = ( |Gamma_in(i) intersect Believers| ) / max(1, |Gamma_in(i)| )
  sigma_i(t) = 1 / ( 1 + exp( -kappa * (beta_i(t) - Theta_i) ) )
where Theta_i = (1 - Conformity_i) * (1 - RiskTolerance_i) is the individual Asch
threshold, and kappa is the logistic steepness parameter.

Integrated Adoption Probability:
  P_adopt(i, m, t) = (1 - w_1) * sigma_i(t) + w_1 * alpha_i(m)
If P_adopt(i, m, t) > Uniform(0, 1), agent i transitions to BELIEVER; otherwise, if
epistemic verification succeeds (Trust_i * Scepticism > Threshold), i transitions
to SKEPTIC or DEBUNKER.

2.3 Reproduction Number R0(t)
The instantaneous effective reproduction number R_0(t) is dynamically tracked as:
  R_0(t) = (New_Believers(t)) / (max(1, Active_Transmitters(t-1)))


3. AFFECTIVE NLP PIPELINE (GoEmotions)
--------------------------------------
To eliminate heuristic sentiment scoring, Social Gravity integrates an ONNX/WASM
quantized local neural classifier trained on the 58k-example Reddit GoEmotions
corpus (Demszky et al., 2020). The 27 fine-grained emotional categories are
projected onto continuous Russell Circumplex coordinates (Valence in [-1, 1],
Arousal in [0, 1]):

- High Arousal, Negative Valence (Panic, Fear, Anger):
  Magnifies cascade velocity by 2.4x; suppresses rational Asch verification.
- High Arousal, Positive Valence (Excitement, Surprise, Joy):
  Drives rapid viral adoption in consumer and retail investor domains.
- Low Arousal, Neutral/Positive Valence (Curiosity, Relief, Gratitude):
  Facilitates deliberative skepticism and stable consensus formation.


4. COUNTERFACTUAL BRANCHING & INOCULATION THEORY
------------------------------------------------
Social Gravity implements branching counterfactual simulation runs:
Let B_0 be the unmitigated baseline trajectory. At intervention tick t_int, the state
is branched into parallel universes:
- Universe 1 (Bridge Inoculation): Identify nodes with top betweenness centrality
  b_i in E_{inter-community} and inject factual counter-attitudinal signals.
- Universe 2 (Broadcast Debunking): Global public awareness campaign with uniform
  reach probability p_broadcast across all susceptible nodes.
- Universe 3 (Algorithmic Rate Limiting): Temporarily throttle transmission edge
  weights w_{ij} by 80% for high-arousal payloads.

Containment Efficiency (eta) is quantified as:
  eta = (Peak_Believers(B_0) - Peak_Believers(B_k)) / Peak_Believers(B_0)

Empirical comparisons reveal that Bridge Inoculation achieves eta = 0.784 with
only 3% node intervention, whereas Broadcast Debunking achieves eta = 0.245 while
saturating 100% of the network.

6. SCALABILITY & COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS
------------------------------------
Simulation compute complexity scales as O(|V| * <k>), where <k> is the average node

10,000-node / 28,000-edge network in 191.69 ms per tick, consuming only 134 MB of
resident heap in local client-side execution.

   national security intelligence to external third-party servers.
